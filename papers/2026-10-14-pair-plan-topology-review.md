# Pair discovery plan: scene and consumer review

2026-10-14. **Accepted; no blocking finding.** Reviewed
[PAIR_DISCOVERY_PLAN.md](../docs/PAIR_DISCOVERY_PLAN.md) against the accepted
[design review](2026-10-14-pair-design-topology-review.md).

`pair_scene.py` owns one complete consumer invariant: bounded selected snapshots
and face keys, canonical chart order, verified actual CPU roots, outward
common-world bounds, retained source identities, index ownership and immutable
page decoding. Native lifetime and callback admission remain one separate
runtime unit. Independent Fraction truth belongs to the oracle unit. The plan
does not scatter geometric enclosure or source binding across product owners.

The page consumer copies words and named metadata before successful child
cleanup and retains the exact scenes. Lazy survivors use examined page-local
bits and the row-major flat offset; padding and omitted pages cannot become
exclusion claims. The root-owned caller walks every page and qualifies every
returned bound and source, making full selected-scene coverage a delivered
task rather than an isolated mask demonstration.

The qualification targets attack the concrete scene faults: reused local face
IDs across bodies, equal local boxes with separated world geometry,
different-frame world coincidence, carrier false positives and exact snapshot
identity. The direct 2^32 total-pair terminal case checks count arithmetic
without allocating or traversing the full product. Publication, construction
and BaseException cleanup cases cover the consumer's ownership boundary.

Small and larger mixed-body scenes exercise useful shortlist production.
Setup, CPU root construction/world conversion, upload, native page, download,
cleanup, decoding, export and caller wall costs remain distinct. Separate event
profiling and initial exact qualification avoid inflating the reused predicate
timing. The plan promises a qualified all-pairs baseline and measures whether
it accelerates the task; it does not claim a BVH, intersection or Boolean.

Proceed to the missing-operation red and explicit implementation authorization.
This review performed source inspection only, with no product edits or native
execution.
