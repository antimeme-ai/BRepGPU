## Independent original-source truth

Point truth reads world extents from original vertices and radial bounds from
the original rim curves, rather than calling the product local-frame predicate.
It rejects infeasible points before collecting all actual face IDs. Rectangle
truth reads original controls/frame, generates inward polygon half-spaces and
enumerates exact rank-three support intersections. Coplanar degenerate segments
and points still acquire complete candidates from the plane plus independent
boundary normals; perpendicular planes acquire endpoints from either finite
face's remaining boundary. Parallel-distinct planes reject every candidate.

All retained candidates satisfy BOTH planes and all closed half-spaces. Exact
Cramer solves use nonzero rational determinants. Deduplicated corner sets give
affine rank; the first difference is nonzero when multiple points exist.
Independent affine inverses check actual world/bothUV values, complete vertex
cardinality/set and left-chart positive turns. This does not replicate product
interval max/min construction. Original geometry checks are the oracle; a
successful process, product parity or attractive SVG is not substituted.
