# Original imported saved-output reader closure

2026-10-17. Implements docs/adaptive-imported-reader-unit.md after parent plan
review. This is test-only original-source qualification of retained outputs.
Native meshing completion, rendering and this Fraction scan are separate
measured operations. No new production evaluator, dependency or native test
campaign was added.

tests/native_adaptive_original_source.py compiles original knot-span POWER
coefficients by Cox level DP once per source span. It reads literal homogeneous
controls, original knots and multiplicities, including nonclamped natural
domains. Original curve endpoints and full surface shared edges establish exact
C0. Rational actual-facet/chord proofs form N-WL before taking a coefficient
hull. Original-span clipping and virtual tightening carry the SAME emitted L;
virtual vertices never sample the source. Analytic source terms retain signed
radii, original frames and literal harmonic arguments. Existing Taylor
enclosures and derivative interpolation establish whole-facet/chord bounds.
The existing finite cap source-quad proof remains unchanged.

tests/native_adaptive_component.py routes original source point, chord, trace
and facet checks through that service, validates source inventories/frontiers,
and reads retained complete-model outputs. Its optional world check composes
original usages exactly, checks actual scene geometry and matrices, and proves
the original operator/storage error bound with an exact square-sum comparison.
tests/native_adaptive_curves_caller.py delegates general NURBS restriction to
the same coefficient service. No sampled parity result accepts geometry.

The algebra follows the plan's previously studied derivative interpolation
(Filip/Magedson/Markot 1986, §2 and Theorems 2/4), rational quotient structure
(Floater 1992, §§1–3), Bernstein subdivision (Ma/Hewitt 2002, §§3.6 and 4.1–4.4)
and original-source error composition (Balazs/Guthe/Klein 2004, §§4–7).
Normalized Cox POWER contraction, simplex monomial conversion and exact world
square-sum comparison are owned derivations, independent of native caches.
