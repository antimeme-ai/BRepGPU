## Direct serialized relation truth

Decoded all three actual submitted binary arrays and five returned arrays with
exact byte extents. Checked metadata against declared primitive counts and
host/device byte formulas. Source tables contain complete unique typed keys,
one body/revision per instance and all declared edge/face/coedge IDs. Permuted
slots are interpreted through these actual source records, not through numeric
entity-ID assumptions.

The expected signed uses come from the declared outer/cap/hole/wall loop tables.
Verified each serialized coedge's edge/face/sign, original loop/hole role and
exact UV-map kind/data against that literal relation. All CSR rows have exactly
their two original uses with correct multiplicities; membership contains every
submitted coedge exactly once. Reader kinds, actual mate/coedge and neighbor/
face slots and full typed output keys match the opposite use independently.
The cylinder coedge4↔6 and plate coedge27↔29 remain distinct uses sharing one
wall face. Hole rim pairs retain their cap-hole and inward-wall provenance.

For the four callers together, this directly checks60 edges,32 faces,
120 coedge occurrences,36 original directed loops and four same-face pair
rows. Counts and consumed source sums agree with actual typed rows, while those
sums remain consumption markers rather than the correctness oracle.

## Original topology and closing-edge distinction

Original edge and face TSVs contain complete unique typed identities, not merely
the right row counts. Checked all original edge endpoint IDs and exact curve
data against canonical vertex/ring/seam declarations. Eight circular rims have
equal endpoint vertex IDs and correct radius1/4 at z=±1/2, while all four
periodic seam edges have distinct bottom/top endpoints. Their reader behavior
comes from retained coedge occurrence semantics, not endpoint heuristics.

Face role/kind/orientation, chart IDs and face-to-loop IDs match each body's
declared records. Wall chart counts remain four charts on one logical face;
they do not produce extra edge uses. Saved loop order remains the original
directed coedge-ID order after slot permutations. Exact hex-encoded UV values
were converted to Fraction and compared with literal line/circle/wall/seam
representations, including cylinder seam u=4/+ and u=0/- and plate's reversed
inward-wall seam uses.
