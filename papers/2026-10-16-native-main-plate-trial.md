and the reproduced difficult 8,300-point spring boundary. That result does not
finish the production mesher. The first actual complete main-plate consumer
trial **hit its predeclared 300-second construction limit**. It produced no
complete qualified part, watch or rendering. Do not use the isolated domain
time as a complete-part speedup.
