# Intersection return-pass options

Research backlog only; no optimization/integration lane starts before the broad
pass big rocks. Keep the first analytic representation and sampled consumer
complete before choosing performance work.

* Cache exact canonical support/coefficient values and domain restrictions by
  exact snapshot/revision/frame and source ordering; never reuse typed lineage
  or GPU handles across ownership domains merely because coordinates agree.
* Deduplicate repeated identical patch/UV queries with a proven scatter map,
  while retaining every seam occurrence. Compare saved evaluation/transfer
  against CPU hashing/gather cost on an actual caller.
* Move bulk curve parameter/UV generation and compact residual consumption to
  owned C++/CUDA only if whole-caller measurements identify them as material.
  Compare reuse of degree(p,0) patches against a minimal curve evaluator; keep
  exact CPU topology/branch classification independent of either execution path.
* Retain session/patch/input ownership deliberately; output pooling needs exclusive
  leases through live result lifetime. The existing lifetime study covers plugin,
  stream and buffer attribution; do not hide final drain or infer unload cost.
* General marching/interval SSI, oblique supports and cylinder/cylinder algebraic
  cases are distinct geometric return passes. Their singular/overlap topology
  and complete coverage must be designed before tuning a numerical trace.

No hardware speedup, library adoption, generic extraction or method selection
is implied by these notes.
