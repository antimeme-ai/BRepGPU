**Disposition: no unresolved geometry blocker.** The three methods are valid conservative lower constructions under their reviewed elementary interval operations. Two early math corrections are integrated: DOP takes the maximum with the summed AABB bound; Bernstein C0 coefficients may be negative. Baseline delivery and all-three-mode whole-operation comparison can proceed to plan review after the small canonical details below are recorded. ## Resolved mathematical attacks

1. **Max slabs can weaken AABB.** Singleton origin, target(3,4,0): AABB sum gives25 while max13 slab distance² is49/2. Design now retains AABB and takes maximum with directional bounds. It correctly avoids calling that maximum exact point-to-DOP distance.
2. **Nonnegative functions can have negative coefficients.** Degree(1,0), Ax=s-1/2,W=1: C0 degree2 Bernstein coefficients are(1/4,-1/4,1/4). With targetX=1/4, N coefficients are(9/16,-3/16,1/16), and true nearest distance is0. Clamping the negative C0 coefficient before reconstructing N would give a false positive lower1/16. Design explicitly forbids it, requires active C4>0, ignores padded zeros, and clamps only the final lower to0.
3. **Approximate XYZ is not a feasible-point certificate.** Constant H=(1,0,0,3) and targetX=round(1/3) have a nonzero exact distance even when the ordinary XYZ subtraction is0. Original-input interval evaluation at reported binary64 UV correctly establishes U. Rank, saddle, search/iteration-limit refinements may propose a usable UV but do not certify exclusion.
4. **Gap and traversal coverage are separate.** Capped traversal uses root lower and can legitimately report GAP_SATISFIED with VISIT_LIMIT and no COVERAGE_COMPLETE. Full traversal can retain a wide bracket. Gap success promises squared-objective precision only; continua and multiple UVs are permitted. Refinement caps continue bounding and do not become visit caps.
5. **Cache provenance and admission are whole-object obligations.** Actual resident arrays are downloaded; source patch snapshots are retained. A failed admission rejects the entire hierarchy rather than omitting patches. Degree(0,0) uses one root/leaf, and other degree-zero axes split only the varying axis. Direct topology/extent oracle must respect these explicit special cases.

## Concrete remaining canonical details

These are small specification clarifications, not objections to the mathematical architecture:

- State the initial split axis when both degrees are nonzero (recommended U at root, then V). Specify left-first or right-first when computed child lower values tie (recommended left-first). The draft says deterministic alternating/near-first, but these ties/start order affect exact work-counter fixtures and limited refinement order. Either convention is correct geometrically; one convention should be the public algorithm recipe.
- For Bernstein cache download specify active coefficient index `i*(2q+1)+j`, first K contiguous pairs in each49-pair block, pair lower then upper, and block order C0,C1,C2,C3,C4. If a7×7 padded physical grid is instead intended, state that actual layout; tests must not infer it. Current wording suggests firstK contiguous but lacks the formula. Do the same in the canonical header/wrapper handoff.
- A depth-bounded13-entry stack suffices for the complete binary depth<=12 build. Unexpected stack overflow is an invariant/arithmetic failure, not normal visit-budget truncation or a sound coverage reason. Parent has already identified and is correcting the draft's generic “visit/stack limitation” fallback wording.
- Strict `LB>U` pruning is observable only if the real predicate or deterministic prune/refinement counters are exercised. One representative and a bracket need not change when a mutant prunes a tied minimizer with `>=`; analytic distance truth alone cannot detect this fault. A direct numerical helper qualification on valid equal operands is enough and reuses the existing owned numerical-test unit. Do not manufacture public candidate enumeration to expose it.

## Exact direct qualification for all three modes

The hierarchy oracle uses original homogeneous Bernstein-to-power expansion, exact dyadic affine restriction and power-to-Bernstein conversion. It does not mirror product de Casteljau. Fraction comparisons require every downloaded AABB enclose all exact restricted Cartesian control ratios. Integer cell/root/child fields establish a complete partition and retained source binding. Child boxes/supports nest if parent intersection is promised.

DOP adds exact Fraction min/max of the restricted-control projections along the ten canonical extra integer axes, with norms squared2 or3. Query-side exact box/slab lower bounds are reduced over all leaves for full coverage or the root for capped fallback. Neither sampling nor CPU parity substitutes for support enclosure. Test negative axes, zero-width support, opposite-sign cancellation, target-dot rounding and norm division.

Bernstein adds exact C0..C4 coefficients for each exact restricted net. The binomial-product identity provides exact coefficient expectations; alternatively exact power-polynomial convolution and degree-(2p,2q) conversion avoids repeating native product-loop order. Select one direct exact construction as the implemented oracle, not a second oracle merely to certify the first. Check every active coefficient interval, C4 positivity, zeros in all inactive pairs, source degree-specific K and all node/block/pair offsets. For a target, exact N=C0-2sum X_k Ck+sum X_k² C4 and exact ratio coefficients qualify the method's ideal lower. The actual cached interval uncertainty may weaken production lower; it must never justify exceeding the exact objective coefficient bound. Combine with exact emitted AABB distance in the flat node/leaf oracle.

At returned binary64 UV, exact original-patch value supplies D(witness); require U>=D with no numerical allowance. For complete AABB/DOP/Bernstein coverage, the method-specific flat exact leaf lower bounds cap the reported L. For visit-limit root fallback, the exact method-specific root bound caps L. Approximate XYZ/descriptive distance get their explicitly reviewed numerical allowances separately. Analytic cases also require L<=known global minimum<=U and actual witness squared error<=tolerance when gap satisfied. No universal UV accuracy is inferred from a small squared gap.

Positive Bernstein product factors are rational, not necessarily binary-exact; e.g. bicubic factors involving denominator20 need proper factor enclosure. C4 exact coefficients are in[1,65536] because all positive weight products lie there and each product coefficient is a convex average. C0 and C1..3 do not inherit nonnegative coefficient ranges. Do not apply a nonnegative-function range to their control coefficients.

## Deterministic and delivered-value corpus targets

- All16 degree pairs and asymmetric rational weights for cache construction, including degree-zero/constant cases; default depth8 and a simple varying-axis maximum-depth12 case. Exact subnormal numerator midpoint2^-1075 directly attacks enclosure rather than underflowed distance.
- DOP aligned diagonal plane S=(s,s,t), target(3/4,1/4,1/2): true d²1/8 versus AABB root0. Norm3 plane S=(s-1/2,t-1/2,1-s-t), target(1/4,1/4,1/4): true d²3/16 versus root0. Neither case requires a nearest-UV solver to derive global truth.
- Rational quarter-cylinder axial target(0,0,1/2): exact d²1 with a continuum of UV minima. Scalar root ratio coefficients in t are(5/4,3/4,5/4); after one t midpoint split both halves have coefficient minimum1. Compare depths0,2,4,8 on the full owned operation. Ordinary outward cache slack is expected; a small sensible positive tolerance should expose the concrete shallow precision benefit without requiring zero-width binary64 intervals.
- Negative C0 line fixture above; constant1/3 false-upper fixture; point-origin(3,4,0) weak max-slab fixture; rational endpoint-local competition, equal endpoint ties, parabola twin minima/saddle/quartic-flat, folds and rank-refused seed cases. These exercise correctness and useful difficult work rather than invalid-query rejection.
- Root refinement counts exactly once; root-leaf duplicate solve is skipped, including depth0 varying patches. Cap0 sets REFINEMENT_LIMIT when the root solve is withheld, still preserves initial witness and bounding. Root/cell visit counter bounds and child-pair visit admission need exact limit fixtures. Invalid slots precede target validation with zero counters/NaN fields under the canonical runtime rule.
- Four gap/coverage/visit-limit combinations and independent refinement-limit presence, including capped-tight and complete-wide. Success does not report all minimizers, and graphical boxes are cached bounds unless actual traversal data is exposed.
