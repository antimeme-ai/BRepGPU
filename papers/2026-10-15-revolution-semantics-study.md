## Useful complete family and actual source placement

Reuse factory PlanarProfile in its **actual local XY plane**: original point (r,a,0), existing /256 grid and strict convex CCW 3..32-vertex boundary, original immutable Frame. Require every original radial coordinate r≥1/4, retaining the existing r≤3/4 and |a|≤3/4 bounds. Convexity then keeps the entire profile away from the axis. Revolve around local **positive Y through the Frame origin**:

`F(tau,v)=(r(v) C(tau), a(v), −r(v) S(tau))`.

At tau0 this is exactly the original source edge, without moving its Y into world Z. Shene's author-hosted revolution notes explicitly distinguish rotating actual profile geometry from rotating a scene; our choice avoids needing any such placement transform. General axes, axis contact/crossing, poles, curved/nonconvex profiles, holes and partial sweeps are separate families.

The map from the closed profile P times a circle into the solid is injective modulo angular periodicity because radius stays positive. Its boundary is ∂P×S1, a complete connected embedded genus-one shell. Full revolution has **no end caps**. The bounded radius makes every chart nonsingular, including annular planar faces generated by horizontal source edges. Vertical/sloped edges generate cylinders/cones without requiring separate canonization or face kinds.

## Rational atlas, coefficients and native truth

For tau=q+s, q=0..3 and s∈[0,1], rotate the quadrant-zero pair
`C=(1−s²)/(1+s²), S=2s/(1+s²)` by q right angles. Each chart includes both endpoints. Four closed quarter images cover the entire circle; do not use a single unbounded rational chart and quietly omit its limiting direction. Sendra/Sevilla/Villarino's primary missing-set paper explains why algebraically valid revolution parametrizations can leave geometric subsets uncovered; our explicit finite closed atlas settles coverage directly for this family.

The degree2 homogeneous quarter controls are `(A,B,W)=(1,0,1),(1,1,1),(0,2,2)`. Tensor with the two original meridian edge endpoints (r,a): each degree2×1 U-major/V-fast control is `(r*A,a*W,−r*B,W)` after the right-angle rotation of (A,B). The denominator is positive. All coefficients are exact dyadics, weights1/1/2; no irrational middle weight, fitting or new rounding is required. Standard equal-endpoint-weight quarter-circle constructions often use sqrt(2)/2, as Shene's circle notes show; a different rational parameter avoids that coefficient here.

Actual native admission checks W∈[1,256] and |homogeneous XYZ|≤W (runtime.cpp), so weighted components up to1.5 at W2 remain supported. Preserve original Frame, fresh output Lineage and raw Patch.uv=(q,q+1,0,1). Actual native queries remain normalized local (u,v)∈[0,1]², with raw tau=q+u. Exact coefficient representation does not make native/world evaluation exact.

Adjacent quarter positions and unit normals agree, but the angular speed changes from1 at the end of one quarter to2 at the beginning of the next under this parameter choice. Do not assert raw first-jet continuity across the atlas. Native first jets must match their actual local rational parameter. The outward normal is proportional to `(da*C,−dr,−da*S)`, so its unit vector requires only sqrt(dr²+da²), independent of radius and v. Integer edge deltas and rational isqrt intervals give independent expected component intervals; multiplying by exact circle components handles non-axis and sloped faces. Radial positivity and minimum source edge length bound Jacobians away from zero.

## Physical topology, maps and exact readers

Each original source vertex supplies one original-position anchor and one closed orbit ring. Each source edge supplies one actual theta0 meridian seam and one whole swept face with four charts. A face's outward raw boundary word is:

1. start ring+, intrinsic UV=(4t,0);
2. own seam+ at tau4, UV=(4,t);
3. end ring−, intrinsic UV=(4t,1);
4. own seam− at tau0, UV=(0,t).

Both seam occurrences belong to the same face, remain distinct and share one physical source-edge copy. Adjacent source edges share their ring by **original vertex ID**. Quarter joins are atlas boundaries, not new physical edges, faces or anchors. Every ring has two ordinary opposed uses; every seam has two opposed same-face uses. An anchor link has **four endpoint germs**: both ends of its closed ring plus incoming/outgoing meridian seams. Verify one cyclic link and connected face graph, not the extrusion's three-port link.

Counts: V=N, E=2N, coedges=4N, loops=N, faces=N, charts=4N, total13N six-kind entities; V−E+F=0. Annular planar faces may use the same complete periodic fundamental rectangle and self-paired seam as other swept faces. No general trim/mesh inference from a canonical planar face is allowed.

Use explicit owned `circle_y` curve records for XZ rings at fixed Y; existing Curve('circle') is an XY circle at fixed Z and must keep that meaning. Ring t∈[0,1] maps tau=4t, with t1 assigned the terminal point of quadrant3. Seams preserve source line geometry/parameter. Exact coedge UV is intrinsic and independent of occurrence sign. Native trim labels mark v0/v1 and the physical tau0/4 seam as BOUNDARY; internal quarter joins at interior v are INTERIOR. Point-material boundary support concerns geometric faces: an anchor lies on its two adjacent swept faces, even though four topological germs meet there.

For exact world-point material membership, invert the original Frame using Fractions. Let local query be (X,Y,Z), Q=X²+Z². A source directed edge starting at(r0,a0) with increments(dr,da) has support `B+C√Q`, where B=dr(Y−a0)+da*r0 and C=−da. Determine sign exactly: handle Q0 and zero/same-sign terms directly; for opposite nonzero signs use sign(B)·sign(B²−C²Q). Unconditional squaring loses signs and is wrong. This requires no rounded sqrt or new algebraic dependency. All nonnegative supports give closed membership; return every zero supporting face, including both faces at a ring and excluding the axis.

Exact volume is π times `scale³/3 * Σ (r_i+r_j)(r_i*a_j−r_j*a_i)`, from the radial first moment. Tight local bounds are X,Z∈[−max(r),max(r)] and Y∈[min(a),max(a)]. These supplement complete source-domain/map equality; correct volume/Euler/samples alone cannot certify the shell.

## What the pinned references actually do

OCCT MakeRevol offers finite/full-turn sweep plus first/last/generated history, and explicitly handles degenerate edges at the axis. Its wrapper calls BuildCurves3d on the source before construction, so a disposable reference copy is prudent even with Copy selected. Rotation's directing-edge parameters are angles; generating parameters survive, and carrier canonization can produce planes/cylinders/cones/spheres/tori or generic revolution surfaces. Native raw coordinates/order are not normalized semantic identities. Actual Generated dispatch checks source participation and generated result support, including replacement history for degenerate copies. We exclude the axis machinery rather than pretend our simple family implements it.

OCCT's actual circle converter cites Tiller1983 and writes the tangent-half-angle rational circle formula; its generic parameterizations may introduce sqrt2 changes of variable. Read the converter and real regression declarations for periodicity, degree/positive weights and endpoint checks. Those upstream tests do not establish our whole topology or alpha qualification.

Truck rsweep selects closed sweep for a full turn; the closed face path connects original boundary wires around the cycle and has no end caps. It may refine rotation into several topological sections, unlike our deliberate one-face-per-source-edge representation. Its geometry decorator uses source-curve parameter first and angular radians second, opposite our angular-U/meridian-V ordering, and has special axis endpoint normals. Normalize coordinate order/orientation before comparing; do not copy derivative signs blindly.

NVIDIA rotational_sweep takes degrees and a point/direction axis; full360 ignores end caps. Its public C++ wrapper copies source curves before invoking the consuming internal primitive and publishes a new Brep only on success. A future optional installed-runtime adapter must use the original world XY source curves, Frame-origin world Y axis and full360, probe source retention and compare actual finite boundary/bounds/volume. It supplies no invented material/history oracle.
