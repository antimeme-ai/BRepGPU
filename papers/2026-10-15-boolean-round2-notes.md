# Boolean/history after the breadth sweep

The first strict through-bore family keeps original source carriers and exact
trim/section/arrangement identity. Broaden geometry and placement as distinct
units after the remaining feature themes. These are notes, not selected work.

* Off-axis/axial tool translations require explicit output curve centers/source
  maps and rigorous frame handling. Preserve exact input predicates and original
  source carrier parameters rather than normalizing away cropping.
* Partial/tangent/coincident-cap cuts require contact semantics and arrangements
  beyond separated circles inside rectangular caps. A complete result for one
  strict family does not establish complete general Boolean topology.
* Interacting cutters and general union/intersection require complete region
  decisions, cross-face event identity, nonmanifold-policy decisions and history
  composition. A deleted source may still generate descendants. Do not reduce
  history to surviving faces or pointer equality.
* Native/GPU construction, certified filters and exact fallback need complete
  event/arrangement/material/topology invariants under scheduling. The existing
  GPU first-jet/adjacency consumers do not migrate those construction stages.
* CutSolid is deliberately separate from canonical BodySnapshot. A generic result
  mesher and broader reader/import integration must implement the actual retained
  domains and source parameters. Do not manufacture canonical plate tags to get
  existing readers to accept the result.
* Persistent naming, incremental edits and source history across sessions remain
  open. Current typed keys bind immutable retained snapshots/revisions.

Whole-operation measurements, and the later decision about Python's shipping
role, should select integration/placement work after the broad pass. Preserve
complete cold setup, cleanup, exact host work and qualification costs. The
operator's NCSX timing comparison is still deferred. Applicable external Boolean
community corpus replay/adapters remain mandatory before alpha; a like-sized
optional reference comparison and local exact oracles do not waive that debt.
