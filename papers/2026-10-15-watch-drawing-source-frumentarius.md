## Finding

**Openmovement OM10 is the strongest route to a real, documented, reusable movement.** Its public technical sheet already supplies actual gear counts, modules, nominal movement dimensions and the stated license. Its current download index lists STEP assemblies. Those STEP downloads require a member login; the free User tier advertises access to constructions. The assembly itself was **not acquired or inspected**, so this study cannot claim a complete manufacturing TDP, a verified CAD BOM, internal pivot coordinates or all Z heights.

The maker's [timeline](https://openmovement.org/timeline-om10/) explicitly reports a ticking first OM10 MAN prototype on 27 May 2023 and ongoing assembly in 2024. This is primary evidence of an actual built movement, rather than just a rendering. Dates such as “31/09” also appear on the timeline; retain the page's chronology without silently correcting those entries. The historical 2021 fundraising dossier alone would not establish a working physical prototype.

## OM10 detail worth engineering against

The [technical sheet](https://openmovement.org/documents/OM10/OM10_Technical_Information_20210314.pdf) p2 specifies the time train under **NIHS 20-02 or NHS 56702/56703**. Barrel 107 teeth drives a 16-leaf pinion at module 0.1300; the following wheels/pinions are 75/10 and 72/9 at module 0.1100, then 84/8 at module 0.08000; the escape wheel has 20 teeth. These data give kinematic and nominal pitch-size constraints. They do not select a complete tooth standard/profile or establish all manufactured geometry. Do not replace these horological teeth with a generic involute because a gear library makes that convenient.

[Release notes](https://openmovement.org/documents/OM10/OM10_Release_notes.pdf) mention wheel-sheet thickness changes from 0.15 to 0.20 mm and pivots changing from 0.167±0.003 to 0.190±0.003 mm. These are practical source-driven stressors for thin geometry, narrow holes and explicit tolerances. They are revision notes, not permission to assign those values to every wheel. One release-note heading repeats 2021/02/01 out of chronological order; use an acquired construction's revision rather than guessing the typo's intended date.

Current [download index](https://openmovement.org/member-downloads/) lists MAN VA 01.3 dated 1 July 2022, earlier beta revisions, MAN VB, AUT beta 01.3 dated 22 May 2023 and a printable holder. A request to the visible MAN VA link redirected to the [membership page](https://openmovement.org/membership-levels/), which advertises a free User tier with constructions access. No STEP bytes were received. The 2021 dossier p6 anticipated PDF drawing release with kit sales and considered a licensing system for electronic resources; that historical statement is not a present catalog of downloadable manufacturing drawings.

## Recommendation

Acquire the OM10 MAN VA construction through the normal free account and inspect **one actual simple wheel blank or bridge**, including its revision, units, component ID, thickness, holes, placements and terms. Select the component after reading that geometry, not by assigning a plausible silhouette from a service image. A wheel blank is a promising first unit because thin extrusion plus real bore and aperture geometry directly tests the next useful kernel capabilities; toothed perimeter and arbor follow only when their source is complete. A bridge is a good alternative if its unambiguous dimensioned section is easier to obtain.

Until the construction is acquired, there is enough public material to write a dimension/source inventory and identify limits, but not enough to truthfully promise a faithful whole movement. For a completely public engineering-drawing fallback, the MIYOTA setting stem and Sellita casing clamps are concrete bounded parts, although neither supplies the open reuse position of OM10 and both introduce their own modeling needs. A render traced from a service drawing should be labeled an approximation, not a manufactured replica.
