The first unit establishes exact rational witness membership on owned solids
and complete closed rectangular-face contact. Continue uncovered arrangements,
meshing, interchange, solid operations and feature themes before strengthening
this family. Correctness fixes and demonstrated blockers retain the roadmap
exception. Python's shipping role is a separate deferred
[architecture investigation](2026-10-14-python-role-return-pass.md).

Future conceptual units, selected only when their later users require them:

- Algebraic intersection-point membership with original symbolic construction
  retained. An exact predicate on a rounded endpoint classifies the rounded
  point; it does not establish the original endpoint's material side. Existing
  Scalar fields cover some quadratics, not arbitrary mixed algebraic extensions.
- Curved-trim overlap and coincidence on disk/hole-cap/cylindrical faces.
  Shared carriers and bounding-box overlap cannot stand in for finite set truth.
  A circle arrangement must preserve exact contacts and distinguish a shared
  span from two representations of one physical boundary.
- Smooth tangency, nearly coincident carriers, oblique/general imported geometry
  and singular branch semantics. The delivered crossing/touch labels concern
  finite rectangles, not all plane/wall or solid contact.
- A separately owned native/GPU sign filter with certified outward bounds,
  unresolved rows and exact fallback retaining query/source ordering. Arbitrary
  finite binary64 includes exponents where fixed-width arithmetic or ordinary
  products cannot establish exact radial signs. Measure fallback-heavy boundary
  inputs and complete publication costs before claiming acceleration.
- Bulk downstream native region consumers, application-lifetime inputs and
  allocation reuse, coordinated with the deferred integration/language decision.
  Preserve immutable live output, failure atomicity and complete boundary-face
  provenance. Pure point throughput does not establish a Boolean speedup.
