# Adjacency design review dispositions

Three independent research and adversarial review lanes examined topology,
runtime/scan/ownership and independent truth. All findings were valid and were
integrated into ADJACENCY_DESIGN before plan review. T1 enforces one body/revision
per occurrence instance with positive uint64 lineage. T2 uses generic same-face
pair/multiple-use labels without claiming arbitrary records are geometric seams
or radially ordered. ADR1 distinguishes E=0 from C=0/E>0; ADR2 delays diagnostic
writes until safe structural alias admission; ADR3 reports failed cleanup rather
than promising impossible reclamation after runtime errors. AO1 establishes full
literal signed-use source/loop/UV truth and independent binary decoding. AO2 adds
measured large global storage permutations. AO3 requires actual CUDA builder and
reader production mutants. No finding expands the geometry scope or adopts a
library. The proposed implementation plan now goes to all three reviewers.
