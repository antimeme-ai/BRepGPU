as the next uncovered broad-pass theme. Three frumentarii are studying topology
semantics, owned CPU/P100 construction/runtime, and independent model oracles.

snapshot lineage and typed entity ID; device arrays carry slots, never pointers.

native execution, qualification, mutations and performance only on the fleet.

polish stays deferred until the broad-pass big rocks are done. No dependency
adoption is proposed. Research precedes design review, plan review and a direct
red test. The fleet's two P100s are idle at the initial read-only inspection.
