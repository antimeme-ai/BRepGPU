# Pair discovery design

Following the operator's breadth-first direction, selected conservative
common-world chart pair discovery as the next complete beachhead. Integrated
three narrow source/oracle studies into docs/PAIR_DISCOVERY_DESIGN.md. Chose
resident immutable bounds, exact finite-bit comparisons, deterministic bounded
mask pages and explicit coverage; retained CPU body bounds/source mapping and
false positives. Submitted the design for independent adversarial review before
planning or product implementation. No new dependencies or native execution.
