# Native deterministic face-cell frontier — 2026-10-16

Scope: one face owner's priority/state consumer of the existing packed mutable topology. This is a prerequisite of the whole native face engine, not a claim that source-to-mesh construction is finished.

A genuine isolated-fleet RED used the authored fixture before `face_frontier.hpp` existed. The implementation maintains a packed per-triangle state table and indexed maximum heap. It uses the topology's cached exact fixed-metric diameter, then stable lineage/triangle identity. A cell has its priority while its result is still Pending. Higher Pending or Unresolved cells block lower ready failures; accepted prefixes retire locally. Frozen contact failures can reinsert an otherwise retired accepted cell. No failure-reason or completion-order priority is introduced.

The frontier consumes `TopologyEdit` deltas. Multiple topology transactions can retire/recycle intermediate inserted generations before their log is consumed, so the fold first resolves slot/generation survival and queries only final live insertions. It never scans all live triangles after an edit. Initialization permits the one initial inventory enumeration.

Identity is explicit: face owner, face generation, source-boundary generation, triangle slot/generation and **request_revision**. The latter is not the bound-policy revision (fixed policy 1 in `BoundTaskHeader`). The numerical owner adds matching request and face-generation envelopes; stale/duplicate completions cannot credit a rebuilt/retried cell.
