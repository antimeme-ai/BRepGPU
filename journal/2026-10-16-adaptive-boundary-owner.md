# Shared physical-boundary owner transactions

2026-10-16. This conceptual unit implements the physical authority portion of
`docs/adaptive-curves-runtime-unit.md`, following the reviewed shared-curve design
and the source/literature study in `papers/native-adaptive-curves-runtime.md`.
The existing DefinitionBoundaryForest remains the sole physical owner; no second
edge identity or refinement controller was introduced.

Before the new methods existed, the authored direct fleet fixture failed to link
precisely on `request_owner_splits` and `stage_owner_promotions`. After implementing
them, the actual original-source fixture passed on the fleet in an isolated
source checkout. Its source is generated by the existing
literal `tests/native_mesher_boundary_fixture.py`; it is not a geometry mock.
The direct executable reported `curve forest GREEN` (exit 0).

The fixture exercises a wire-only owner split combined with a revision of a
shared interior physical point. Both incident faces change generation once.
Wrong logical keys, fixed original-vertex replacement, wrong split completion,
and stale constructed revisions are refused. A staged promotion does not change
installed Q, and a failed completion does not install it. The successful combined
transaction retains the logical key, increments its separate constructed
revision, splits the wire, and preserves the older immutable snapshot.

Every exported boundary node now has a parallel constructed revision. Temporary
exact tables may receive an explicit owner namespace so fresh quality-query
forests cannot reuse exact-table keys while earlier snapshots remain alive.
The default namespace and all PhysicalNodeKey rules are preserved for existing
callers. No geometry is welded or original vertex value replaced.

Sources were formatted using the actual native style, with temporary files and
atomic replacement on the fleet. Root's session/caller and the curve geometry
and occurrence-construction units remain separate in-flight work; this entry
makes no claim that the whole shared-curve operation is complete.

Independent algorithm-owner actual-source review ACCEPT closed after reading the
header/body diff and the direct fixture. It specifically checked live-horizon
wire admission, combined promotion validation, original vertex/quotient refusal,
precommit revision revalidation, deduplicated face generations, failed-evaluation
retry, parallel revision export and exact-table namespace lifetime. No additional
