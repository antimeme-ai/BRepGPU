retained conservative-bound arithmetic and pinned OCCT pair/box source portions.
Saved papers/2026-10-14-pair-topology-study.md. No product code, dependency,
new agents, local native or fleet execution.

Recommended closed-AABB survivor bits over immutable left/right carrier indices
in a common world frame. Strict separation only; touching and singleton boxes
remain candidates. Whole untrimmed bounds avoid trim false exclusions; a bit1
is not an intersection witness. Four cylinder charts retain one face identity.

reuse local-coordinate clipping after transformation.

Paged row-major order names exact covered intervals, index identities and an
unprocessed suffix. start>0 does not silently certify prior pairs; padding is
not exclusion. Checked counts and unique packed-word ownership avoid capacity/
atomic-append truncation ambiguity. Material caller should shortlist multi-part
scenes, reporting overhead/reuse and repeated geometry without SSI claims.
Generic inputs/BVHs/trim narrowing remain separate work; canonical sources make
the first unit attainable. No delivered pair capability is claimed yet.
