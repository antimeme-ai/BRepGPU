## Lifetime and reported units

Mesh hints may be temporarily absent during a delete/add transaction. Main
star lookup occurs only after both completed cavity outputs recreate every
vertex of the deleted straight strip; a vertex strictly inside that strip would
require the constraint to pass through the site, excluded by input validation.
Scratch star lookup occurs only for fresh u after its complete insertion creates
u-incident faces. A selected secondary fan also shares u, so the distinct old
apex excluded from its boundary cannot occur inside that fan. No star operation
is allowed during the deletion window; no global O(E) hint repair is introduced.

quadedge_peak counts live undirected four-record groups. triangle_peak is max
live faces in any one mesh, not aggregate. Shared memory resource accounts all
simultaneous owners. order_ns is a subset of initial_ns; conversion_ns separately
includes face enumeration and recovery-index construction. Whole-call timing
retains owner allocation and all other overhead.
