# Trim-aware meshing breadth beachhead

The operator sent the next delivery. Read current doctrine, theme roadmap,
body/trim contracts and hard external alpha requirement. Selected uncovered
trim-aware meshing before interchange/solid/features and before deferred GPU
residency, integration polish or the Python-role study. Commissioned separate
geometry/reference, independent-oracle and runtime/identity studies.

Research highlights the distinction that will govern the design: straight
triangles approximate circular trims. An inscribed cap omits a thin circular
rim; an inscribed hole polygon includes a thin strip inside the exact void.
Require complete chordal-domain coverage, shared physical boundary identities,
closed oriented incidence and an explicit two-sided world chord bound. Never
substitute sampled trim labels, area/Euler totals or an attractive image for
those claims. Current community-case adapters remain missing alpha release debt.

Study candidate: complete factory box/cylinder/one-hole-plate meshes, CPU exact
construction, rational edge samples and independent whole-face coverage checks.
No new library, general triangulator, GPU lifetime work or native rebuild is
selected. Source studies and reviewed complete design precede implementation.
