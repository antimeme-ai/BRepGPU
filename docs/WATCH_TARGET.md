# Watch-inspired modeling-chain target

Operator direction, 2026-10-15: make the next actual composition demonstration
something unusually compelling, such as a mechanical watch; leave durable notes.
This supplements the continuing NCSX comparison target, not replaces it.

## Intended first scene

An original skeletonized watch-inspired movement: pierced mainplate/bridges,
layered brass wheels, blue structural pieces, ruby-colored bearings, visible
pivots and a balance-like assembly. Make depth and the arrangement of parts
readable, with an oblique hero view and inspectable part/source inventory.
Use real returned solid meshes as rendering input, preserving prior artifacts.
The first scene is a schematic design study, not a functional/manufacturable
caliber or reproduction of an existing watch. Tooth forms, gear ratios,
escapement timing, spring elasticity, tolerances and mating are not established
by an attractive static assembly. Label illustrative choices in the actual output.

## Actual modeling claim

Original2D profile → owned extrusion result → admitted axial bore → bounded
complete mesh. Each stage consumes the actual prior result. Keep original source
carrier controls, Frames, raw UV and complete typed source history. A final face,
edge or seam occurrence must remain traceable through the cut and extrusion to
its original profile and the actual cutter. Existing box-only Boolean admission
is insufficient; do not relabel an extrusion as a canonical box.

The candidate new family is one circular, strictly interior, fully through axial
bore in a strictly convex lattice-profile extrusion. Exact support predicates
can establish whole-circle separation from every profile edge. Admission,
complete closed topology, original cutter UV band, original profile/history
composition and a complete cap-with-hole mesh need their own reviewed design.
A general Boolean engine, interacting/multiple cutters and a full mechanical
watch feature tree are outside this first family.

## Visual scope and honest parts

An assembly may contain many separate modeled solids. Existing convex extrusions
and the new pierced family can supply plates, wheel blanks, bearings and bridges.
If stylized teeth are individual convex extrusions, name them as separate parts;
they do not become one analytically fused gear. Do not fabricate a nonconvex
profile, smooth union, physical gearing or simulation claim to fill the picture.
Any display-only detail must be named separately from modeled geometry.

Preserve component recipes, source IDs/revisions, local/world transforms,
material assignment, camera/light settings and actual indexed triangle data.
Material colors and assembly placement improve legibility; no cosmetic change
alone establishes kernel progress. Record the useful chain and display timing
separately from independent checks, setup/export and optional native consumers.

## Current study notes

The actual existing feature family admits only3..32 strictly convex CCW lattice
vertices and bounded Frames/heights. The actual first Boolean is canonical-box
specific throughout. The next implementation must own the admitted feature cut
and its real trimmed cap domains. A circle inside a convex polygon permits a
visible bridge from its rightmost point to a maximum-X outer vertex; exact
hole-aware triangulation is being studied, with original shared physical indices
and complete positive boundary-chain checks.

## Testing and stopping

Test admitted/unsupported families, complete source maps and composed ancestry,
whole exact/rounded domains, shared boundary/manifold topology, output error and
failed-call preservation. Actual watch parts must pass their appropriate direct
qualifiers. Review actual rendered output and disclose schematic limitations.
Then publish and stop this bounded delivery; no mutation-score, broad numerical
rerun or certification campaign follows just because the image is impressive.
