# M4-G4 brief: Physical embedding near thin and knife-edge features

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G4. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.5`.

**Purpose.** Decide whether the draft's ε-inflated planning query keeps the
output free of self-intersection near thin walls, knife edges, small dihedral
angles and near-tangent trims, and what the construction does when it does not.
Zero forbidden pairs across the watch's 107 definitions is one workload, not
evidence.

**Decision it blocks.** M5 §5.6, and whether alpha needs an in-construction
contact rule.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-features (never drop features
above ε); the fidelity contract (deviation against the repaired target).

**Questions.**
1. Sufficient conditions in the literature for a surface mesh to embed without
   self-intersection given chord error ε: Khoury–Shewchuk, restricted Delaunay
   and ε-sampling results, Li et al. 2026 (JCDE; unread so far, so acquire it),
   and others.
2. Our sufficient condition, with a proof sketch, or a counterexample family
   showing the draft's query is unsound.
3. Adjacent faces at small dihedral angles, near-tangent trims, thin walls
   under 2ε, and knife edges: the failure each produces and the construction
   rule that prevents it (local ε reduction, contact-aware sizing, refusal).
4. Cost: what the check costs per face and whether it batches for SIMD or GPU.

**Measurement.** None, beyond a symbolic or numerical check of your own
derivation.

**Output slug.** `thin-feature-embedding`: `papers/2026-10-17-thin-feature-embedding.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** A stated sufficient condition with proof sketch, or a
counterexample family; the construction rule for inputs that violate it. Every
question has an evidence-backed answer or an explicit "the literature does not
say" with the measurement that would settle it.

**Return.** The condition, the rule, and the families to author. Then the
common return items.
