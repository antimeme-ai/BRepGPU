# M4-G13 brief: Shading fidelity delivered to the consumer

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G13. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.14`.

**Purpose.** The output must look stunning. Tie the angle cap and normal policy
to a bound or a visual criterion (reflection lines), decide G1-continuity
classification when the file does not encode regularity, and decide how normals
reach consumers whose formats cannot carry them.

**Decision it blocks.** M5 §4.7, and the per-format normal policy with
D-formats.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-formats (glTF with normals; STL
and 3MF carry none); D-consumers (one glTF renderer).

**Questions.**
1. The relation between chord error, triangle angle, normal error and visible
   artefacts: reflection lines, highlights and the shadow terminator
   (Khoury–Shewchuk 2019, Shewchuk 2002, Guthe et al. 2006, the shadow-
   terminator papers).
2. A cap or angle rule with a stated basis.
3. G1 classification of an edge when the file has no encoded regularity: the
   angle rule, the band between tangent and crease, and the CAx-IF tangent and
   crease thresholds.
4. The per-format normal policy, and what consumers do with normal-less
   formats.
5. Optional measurement: reflection-line renders of authored families with the
   repository's existing renderer on the fleet, if that answers a question
   cheaply.

**Measurement.** Optional: renders of authored families on the fleet.

**Output slug.** `shading-fidelity`: `papers/2026-10-17-shading-fidelity.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** A cap or angle rule with its basis, a G1 rule, and the per-
format normal policy. Every question has an evidence-backed answer or an
explicit "the literature does not say" with the measurement that would settle
it.

**Return.** The three rules. Then the common return items.
