# M4-G1 brief: What the ingress bridge changes before we see the geometry

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G1. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.2`.

**Purpose.** OCCT 8.0.1's STEP translation layer edits geometry even with
ShapeProcess off: it discards and recomputes planar pcurves, ignores
`master_representation`, merges vertices, reads mirrored placements as
rotations, accepts scaled locations, sets shells `Closed` unchecked and merges
alternative and tessellated representations. Establish the complete list, how
often each fires on real inputs, and what we must read from raw entities
instead.

**Decision it blocks.** M5's ingress section: keep the bridge, keep it with
raw-entity overrides, or bring the owned reader forward. Also the authority
rule when raw and translated disagree, and whether the steel-manned NVIDIA
arm's input is what we say it is.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-master (follow the declared
master; with no pcurves, recompute them with a recorded residual); D-shapes
(main shape representation only, report the others); D-units; D-placements.

**Questions.**
1. Every translation-layer edit on the STEP read path, independent of
   ShapeProcess flags: the trigger, the effect and the file/function at OCCT
   8.0.1. Start from the map's L2 list (`StepToTopoDS_TranslateEdgeLoop.cxx`,
   `StepToGeom.cxx`, `StepToTopoDS_Builder.cxx`, `DESTEP_Parameters.hxx`,
   `STEPControl_ActorRead.cxx`) and close it: look for every BRepLib, ShapeFix,
   ShapeAnalysis, tolerance update or SameParameter call reachable from
   transfer.
2. Frequency of each on the M3 corpus and the already-held STEP sets, per
   entity, raw entity against translated shape. Examples: pcurve present in the
   file versus in the shape; transform determinant and scale;
   `master_representation` value; shared vertex count before and after;
   representation count per product.
3. A disposition per edit (accept, read raw, refuse) under D-master and
   D-shapes, and one stated authority rule.
4. The steel-man. NVIDIA reads only `.brep`, which we produce through this
   bridge. What is NVIDIA's strongest legitimate input path, and does the
   bridge disadvantage or advantage it?
5. Ingress cost. Per corpus file, the time of the OCCT read and transfer
   against a raw-entity read, since ingress is a stage we must also beat NVIDIA
   at.

**Measurement.** Allowed: an OCCT probe over the corpus on the fleet. No
meshing.

**Output slug.** `occt-ingress-translation-edits`: `papers/2026-10-17-occt-ingress-translation-edits.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** A table of every translation-layer edit with trigger, effect,
corpus frequency and disposition; the authority rule; the steel-man statement;
ingress costs. Every question has an evidence-backed answer or an explicit "the
literature does not say" with the measurement that would settle it.

**Return.** The edit table in brief, the authority rule, and the steel-man
answer. Then the common return items.
