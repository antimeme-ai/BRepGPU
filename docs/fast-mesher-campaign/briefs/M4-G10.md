# M4-G10 brief: Small-feature collapse soundness

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G10. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.11`.

**Purpose.** Show, or refute, that the draft's β-ball edge collapse and
boundary-only face rule keep every emitted triangle within ε, preserve
dimension, disperse symmetrically and record history.

**Decision it blocks.** M5 §5.2 item 5: the thresholds and checks.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-features (never drop features
above ε; defeaturing is out of scope); M2's adopted defaults (transverse leaps
bounded by ¼ local feature size; small tangent gaps mesh as recorded steps).

**Questions.**
1. Per rule (edge collapse, boundary-only face, vertex merge), a bound on the
   deviation of every emitted triangle, or a counterexample family.
2. Dimension preservation: when a collapse turns a face into an edge or a
   point, and how that is recorded and refused.
3. Symmetric dispersal, and history recording, in Sheffer 2000's collapse
   operator, Shephard 1998 validity and Owen 2019's failure rates.
4. Authored families to test each rule: slivers at η/2, η and 2β; curved and
   non-convex small faces.

**Measurement.** None.

**Output slug.** `small-feature-collapse`: `papers/2026-10-17-small-feature-collapse.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** A stated bound or counterexample per rule, and the validity
checks. Every question has an evidence-backed answer or an explicit "the
literature does not say" with the measurement that would settle it.

**Return.** The bound or counterexample per rule. Then the common return items.
