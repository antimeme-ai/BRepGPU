# M4-G18 brief: Post-processing legality and LOD

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G18. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.19`.

**Purpose.** Write the legality rules for operations on the mesh inside the
mesher (edge flips, smoothing, vertex moves, decimation), and place LOD (re-
tessellation at a coarser ε) and decimation (downstream only).

**Decision it blocks.** M5's statement of which operations exist in the mesher.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-features; D-consumers;
D-provisional; the fidelity contract.

**Questions.**
1. For each candidate operation, whether it can preserve every guarantee
   (deviation within ε, manifold, oriented, no new self-intersections, shared
   edge identity), and under what check.
2. LOD by re-tessellation at a coarser ε, against decimation, for the renderer
   and for sharpen-in-place.
3. What stays downstream, and the statement we make to consumers about it.

**Measurement.** None.

**Output slug.** `postprocessing-lod`: `papers/2026-10-17-postprocessing-lod.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** An allowed, forbidden and downstream table. Low risk; much is
settled by the map's L5 lane. Every question has an evidence-backed answer or
an explicit "the literature does not say" with the measurement that would
settle it.

**Return.** The table. Then the common return items.
