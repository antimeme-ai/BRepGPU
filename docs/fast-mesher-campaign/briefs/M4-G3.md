# M4-G3 brief: Export precision and reader-equivalent validity

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G3. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.4`.

**Purpose.** Make the written file, as consumers actually read it, keep the
guarantees the FP64 mesh has: manifold, oriented, no new self-intersections,
deviation within ε. Most consumers store positions in single precision, and
naive rounding creates new self-intersections in a published share of cases.

**Decision it blocks.** M5 §5.5 export and the §10 export oracle. Options:
float32 as declared quantization with a re-import oracle, or snap rounding with
intersection removal. Also welding policy per format, and origin handling for
large assemblies.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-formats: STL; 3MF with separate
items, not united components; glTF with face ids and normals. USD and AP242
tessellated later. D-consumers: PrusaSlicer, Cura, one glTF renderer, fTetWild.

**Questions.**
1. Per format and per named consumer, the re-import procedure: number parsing
   and precision, welding and merge tolerances, repair steps (PrusaSlicer
   admesh, Cura, lib3mf, a glTF loader, fTetWild input). Cite code at pinned
   revisions.
2. Which guarantees survive plain rounding, and which break. Measure on the
   fleet: mesh corpus parts with OCCT BRepMesh as a stand-in producer (not our
   product, and say so), round to float32, and count new self-intersections,
   flipped triangles and lost manifoldness after each consumer's re-import
   emulation.
3. Remedies: snap rounding (Lazard–Valque 2025, held), CGAL snap-rounding
   fixtures, quantization-aware construction (meshing so float32 rounding is
   safe by construction), and recentring origins per item. Cost and guarantee
   for each.
4. 3MF separate items: what the spec and lib3mf require for items that touch or
   overlap.

**Measurement.** Allowed: OCCT BRepMesh stand-in meshes of corpus parts and a
rounding and re-import probe, on the fleet.

**Output slug.** `export-precision-validity`: `papers/2026-10-17-export-precision-validity.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** Per format: the re-import procedure the oracle emulates, the
measured failure rate under plain rounding, and the selected remedy with its
cost. Every question has an evidence-backed answer or an explicit "the
literature does not say" with the measurement that would settle it.

**Return.** The failure rates and the recommended remedy per format. Then the
common return items.
