# M4-G6 brief: Units and parameter-space semantics

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G6. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.7`.

**Purpose.** Read every unit and uncertainty correctly, per representation
context, including the plane-angle unit inside pcurve and analytic parameters.
Lift today's mm-only refusal, since inch and SI-prefixed inputs are common in
CAM and printing.

**Decision it blocks.** Lifting the mm-only refusal, and the unit rules for
later IGES and Parasolid XT readers.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-units (honour declared units;
refuse unit-less input unless the caller states units). B1 fixes the unchecked
plane-angle unit at one site in parallel; G6 supplies the whole table, so read
the B1 brief and do not touch the code.

**Questions.**
1. Per surface and curve kind, every parameter that carries a length or angle
   unit, and how it maps (for example a cone's v and its cos α, trimmed-circle
   parameters in degrees, and pcurves on periodic surfaces).
2. What OCCT converts by itself (`STEPConstruct_UnitContext`,
   `StepData_Factors`, `GeomConvert_Units`) and what reaches our code raw.
3. Per-context uncertainty: files with two contexts, such as NIST FTC-10 with
   0.01 mm on the B-rep and 1e-6 mm on presentation. Which context governs
   which entity.
4. Known pitfalls: double conversion of inch files (the PrePoMax report), OCCT
   0029803, the IGES and XT defaults. The IGES 5.3 PDF was removed for its
   notice; it is on SHOPPING_LIST.md, so work from secondary sources and say
   so.
5. Census of unit contexts in the corpus (text-level).

**Measurement.** Allowed: a text-level census of unit contexts on the fleet.

**Output slug.** `units-parameter-semantics`: `papers/2026-10-17-units-parameter-semantics.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** A unit and parameter-map table per surface kind, the per-context
uncertainty record, and the refusal rules. Every question has an evidence-
backed answer or an explicit "the literature does not say" with the measurement
that would settle it.

**Return.** The table and the refusal rules. Then the common return items.
