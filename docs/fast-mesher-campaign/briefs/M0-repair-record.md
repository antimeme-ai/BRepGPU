before any new research launches, so later maneuvers start from a correct
record instead of a transcript.

**Serves the goal by.** BLACKBIRD makes the repo the shared memory: the
journal, the design's status and the shopping list are what every later lane
and every future colleague reads first.

**Inputs.** The 2026-10-17 journal; docs/FAST_MESHER_DESIGN.md; the
not-acquired sections of the seven 2026-10-17 research papers (literature
§Acquisition notes; CAD test plans "Reproducers worth acquiring" and
Appendix C; mesh-generation test plans Appendix B; growth clusters §7;
healing §Acquisition notes).

1. Journal entry for the halt: what went wrong, what PLAN.md changes.
2. Status line on FAST_MESHER_DESIGN.md: working draft, rewritten whole in M5.
3. SHOPPING_LIST.md at the repository root: every item a lane could not
   acquire, grouped by kind, each with why we want it, where it was sought,

4. One commit on research/fast-mesher-study, pushed.
5. Journal entry closing M0.

**Done when.** Committed and pushed; privacy scrub clean; no background work
running.

**Must not.** Launch any lane; change design content; run compute; publish
hostnames, absolute workstation paths or private operational detail.
