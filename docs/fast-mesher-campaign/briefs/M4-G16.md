# M4-G16 brief: Source identity passthrough and the classification map

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G16. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.17`.

**Purpose.** Carry source identity into every output that can hold it: STEP
entity ids, persistent UUIDs, `shape_aspect` groupings (one hole across two
faces), a geometric link per face, and per-vertex classification including
collapse history. The roadmap (exploded diagrams, mechanisms, physics, editing)
needs to know which triangles came from which face.

**Decision it blocks.** M5 §5.5 output schema and face ids per format.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-formats (glTF with face ids);
D-features; the internal-data rule.

**Questions.**
1. The identity fields and their sources: the CAx-IF PID recommended practice
   v1.8, the cross-domain recommended practice, the 3D tessellated recommended
   practice §5.4, and Shephard 1998 classification.
2. Per-vertex classification (on a face, an edge or a vertex of the source) and
   collapse history.
3. Carriage per format: 3MF triangle sets, glTF custom attributes or
   extensions, USD GeomSubsets, and what STL loses.
4. The cost of carrying identity through the hot path, and how to keep it off
   it.

**Measurement.** None.

**Output slug.** `source-identity-passthrough`: `papers/2026-10-17-source-identity-passthrough.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** The identity fields and their carriage per format. Every
question has an evidence-backed answer or an explicit "the literature does not
say" with the measurement that would settle it.

**Return.** The field list and the per-format carriage. Then the common return
items.
