# M4-G8 brief: Heterogeneous placement and the measurement protocol

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G8. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.9`.

**Purpose.** Choose how work is placed across the fleet host's two sockets and
two P100s, and how timings are taken so that comparisons with NVIDIA are fair
and honest.

**Decision it blocks.** M5 §5.5 scheduling and §9.5 measurement; whether the
GPU is used for meshing work at all, given today's measured cost of about 2.5
ms per face.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-measure (record SMT and AutoNUMA
state; cold and warm; per-definition and per-occurrence accounting); the
operator's timing rule (every timing emits its lane and the competing non-test
load); the steel-man rule; the CPU governor question, still open, so record
frequency.

**Questions.**
1. Placement: HEFT, StarPU performance models, work stealing under
   multiprogramming, and LPT. Which fits a global task graph of edges and faces
   on two NUMA nodes and two GPUs, and what cost model does it need?
2. GPU worth: from the old regime's measured numbers and the literature, which
   stages profit on a P100 (FP64 at 1/2 rate) and at what batch size. State a
   hypothesis with its falsifier, not a conclusion.
3. The intra-face split threshold derived from the critical path T∞.
4. NUMA: first-touch, per-node pools and arenas, and GPU affinity (GPU0 on node
   0, GPU1 on node 1).
5. The measurement protocol: repetitions and statistics (Hoefler–Belli;
   Mytkowicz et al. on measurement bias), warm and cold, process isolation, and
   how NVIDIA's runs are pinned so that they are at their strongest.
6. Hardware microbenchmarks on the fleet: memory bandwidth per node and across
   nodes, PCIe transfer per GPU from each node, kernel launch latency, and
   frequency under load. Record contention; other lanes may be running, so
   repeat when the host is quiet and say which runs were quiet.

**Measurement.** Allowed: hardware microbenchmarks on the fleet. No mesher or
NVIDIA runs.

**Output slug.** `heterogeneous-placement-measurement`: `papers/2026-10-17-heterogeneous-placement-measurement.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** A placement rule with its cost model and fallback, a stated
intra-face split threshold, and a measurement protocol covering accounting,
warm and cold, NUMA and repetitions. Every question has an evidence-backed
answer or an explicit "the literature does not say" with the measurement that
would settle it.

**Return.** The placement rule, the protocol, and the microbenchmark numbers.
Then the common return items.
