# M4-G5 brief: Entity coverage for arbitrary inputs

Read with `M4-common.md`. Stub: `papers/2026-10-17-geometry-lifecycle-map.md` §4.1 G5. Bead: `brepgpu-12s.6`.

**Purpose.** Decide which STEP entities the mesher must accept to mean
"arbitrary B-rep input", and how each is represented.

**Decision it blocks.** The M5 admission matrix: voids, sheets and open shells,
faceted B-reps, surfaces of revolution and extrusion as exact rational
B-splines, offset surfaces with a computed bound, polyline edges, degenerate
tori, hyperbolae and parabolae, tessellated-only parts, and surface degree
above 3.

**Operator decisions that bind this lane.** D-shapes (main shape representation
only; report the others); D-features; D-partial-output. B1 is fixing silently
dropped voids in parallel, so read its brief but do not touch the code.

**Questions.**
1. Entity frequencies over the corpus: NIST, CAx-IF, open-hardware packages,
   the OCCT dataset and step-conformance (ABC is not yet acquired; say so). A
   text-level Part 21 census is enough for most counts; use OCCT only where a
   count needs translation.
2. For each kind, what AP242 and Part 42 allow (longform WR rules) and what the
   reference kernels do with it.
3. The representation for each admitted kind, with its error budget where
   conversion is inexact (offset surfaces), and the cost of supporting it.
4. The refusal record for each kind not admitted, and whether refusing it is
   honest under "arbitrary input".
5. Whether open shells and sheets are meshed (for display and printing) or
   refused.

**Measurement.** Allowed: a text-level census and an OCCT census on the fleet.
No meshing.

**Output slug.** `entity-admission-matrix`: `papers/2026-10-17-entity-admission-matrix.md` and its sources TSV.

**Done when.** An admission matrix with frequency per kind, the representation
for each admitted kind, and the refusal record for the rest. Every question has
an evidence-backed answer or an explicit "the literature does not say" with the
measurement that would settle it.

**Return.** The matrix, most frequent kinds first. Then the common return
items.
