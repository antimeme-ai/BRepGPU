two incorrectly bounded Plane-domain cases. Both adapters are now explicit:

  named Plane `face:range` values in a derivative layer. It retains all other
  typed values, original support geometry and topology. No-Plane input is a
  byte-identical pass-through.
- [Periodic preparation](nvidia-periodic-preparation-unit.md) applies public
  OCCT angle subdivision to a guarded source copy, only after original
  conversion refuses. Exact nominal-period coverage/multiplicity, directed
  chains, face sense and original geometry/vertex/tolerance guards remain.
  Definitions1716/1811 change6→11 and117→122 faces; all original face ancestry
  remains, yielding9,172 prepared faces for9,162 original parents.
  New cut tolerances are disclosed.48 shortened source-endpoint ranges are
  restored to literal original values; stale SameRange/SameParameter flags
  are invalidated. This is not downstream mesh welding or triangle repair.
