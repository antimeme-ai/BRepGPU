## Coarse selected-domain geometry and small features

The selected polygon can be unusable before its approximation error exceeds
positional epsilon. Preparation checks exact working-coordinate inventory before
mutable topology appends sites: fewer than three source intervals in a loop or
nonincident exact coordinate aliases use the retained actual whole-trace
source/reference deviation to request a canonical original master split. This
never welds source IDs, removes a hole, or installs a circle sample count.

The same exact domain validator reports count, zero-area, contact and placement
issues in original loop/segment order. Geometry classifies that report; it does
not run a second crossing validator. Count/zero-area inspect every affected-loop
trace; hole placement inspects the whole face because the enclosing outer curve
can be responsible; contact uses its reported pair. A positive canonical upper
permits bounded refinement. Missing evidence is unresolved. Zero physical deviation does not establish source-chart injectivity or hole
ownership; absent a direct source-extent diagnosis it is unresolved. Declared same-edge/shared-vertex
contact remains an unsupported occurrence/chart issue unless a stronger direct
source argument distinguishes forbidden overlap; it is not automatically called
invalid source. No universal termination claim is made for ambiguous source
geometry. Resource/generation limits still bound repeated master subdivision.

A direct exact-cubic one-edge closed-hole counterfamily has no interior mandatory
knots, repeated original vertex identity, and diameter much smaller than epsilon.
It must progress through the real forest's source-driven splits to at least
three nonzero selected boundary segments and enter the same direct CDT with its
hole retained. This test attacks topology lost by tolerance-only acceptance.
